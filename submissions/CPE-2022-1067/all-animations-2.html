Three scenes in 3D

Real CSS 3D: cubes, depth and a moving camera, all driven by CSS keyframes.

1. The cat and the mouse

The mouse runs circles around the cat while the cat’s head swivels to follow. The camera drifts so you can see the depth.

2. The car on the same axis

The car circles the central pole at the same pivot as the cat and mouse, wheels turning in proportion to its speed.

3. Two men, one conversation

A man without legs, seated in his wheelchair, faces a skeptic in a storm. The camera pushes in when lightning strikes. The scene loops every 21 seconds.

One leg gone. What is left of you?
More than you will ever understand.
The world will not slow down for you.
Then I’ll make my own pace, and arrive anyway.
…Impossible.
I am not what I lost. I am still standing.
